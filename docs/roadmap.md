# Roadmap

## Completed

- Working point of sale
- Supabase authentication
- Atomic checkout and inventory deduction
- Product creation
- Stock receiving and adjustments
- Low-stock monitoring
- Inventory movement history

## Recommended next milestones

1. Admin and cashier roles
2. Product editing, deactivation and barcode support
3. Customer management
4. Suppliers and purchase orders
5. Sales dashboard and reporting
6. Receipt printing
7. Returns and refunds
8. Offline-first transaction queue and synchronization
9. Automated tests

## Production-readiness items

- Separate production Supabase project
- Backup and recovery procedure
- Formal authorization model
- Audit review and exception reporting
- Device and session management
- Tax and receipt requirements review
- Load, concurrency and failure testing
